**Summary.** Of the 19 papers and benchmarks reviewed, only two (Broken by Default, PoCGen) confirm that a vulnerability is actually exploitable at scale, and both answer a different question than this thesis: one proves memory-corruption bugs in C/C++ with a mathematical method, the other exploits already-known CVEs rather than vulnerabilities an AI model freshly introduces. Two more (Yoo & Kim, CWEval) confirm exploitability only partially — for two hand-picked examples, or via execution checks that stop short of a full attacker-controlled exploit path. Three entries (SecurityEval Dataset, PRIMEVUL, Multi-Language SVD) are datasets or detection-only benchmarks where exploit confirmation does not apply. Every remaining paper — twelve in total, spanning Copilot, ChatGPT, and other model families across Java, Python, C/C++, and JavaScript — detects vulnerabilities but never confirms whether the flagged code can actually be exploited. No paper reviewed functionally confirms exploitability for SQL Injection, Command Injection, and Path Traversal specifically, across multiple AI models, in Python, Java, or JavaScript. That is the gap this thesis will address.

## Other Research Gaps

- **Detection methods disagree with each other.** LLM-CSEC: 3 SAST tools, 0 common CWEs. SafeGenBench: SAST/LLM-judge agreed 61% of the time. Yoo & Kim: tools miss 53.3% of AI-code vulns. Motivates using multiple detection methods before exploit confirmation.
- **Vulnerabilities usually buried in broader code-quality studies** (Sabra et al.: only ~2% of findings were vulnerabilities) rather than the primary object of study.
- **Security-focused prompting is inconsistent, sometimes counterproductive** (LLM-CSEC: "secure" persona sometimes produced more vulnerable code) — a confound to watch when designing prompts, not something to solve.
- **Task scope stays small and narrow** across all four earlier studies — single-function/file, small samples (Yoo & Kim: 20 programs), single-model/language in some cases. Worth naming as a boundary of my own study too.
